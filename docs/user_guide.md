# User Guide

## Login
1. Enter username and password.
2. Select role.
3. Click Login.

## Register Patient
1. Click Register Patient.
2. Fill all fields.
3. Click Register.

## View Queue
1. Click View Queue.
2. See waiting patients.

## Call Next Patient
1. Open Queue Dashboard.
2. Click Call Next.

## Search Patient
1. Enter Patient ID or Name.
2. Results appear automatically.

## Logout
1. Click Logout.
